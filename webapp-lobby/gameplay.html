Game Help

How to Play

  • Place Cards: Click or drag cards from your hand to the playfield
  • Mass Select: Hold Shift and click multiple cards; mass‑discard with Delete/D
  • Build Expression: Create a valid expression: Number, then Operator + Number, repeat
  • Score: Click the playfield when your expression equals the target number
  • Discard: Select a card, then press Delete or D to discard it
  • End Turn: Click the timer to end instantly, or wait for timer
  • Rehand: Hold the deck button to discard your hand and draw based on host rule (ends turn)
  • Undo / Clear: Use the buttons under the playfield to undo last card or clear the whole expression
  • Bot: When enabled by host, Player 2 will auto‑draw and pass turns

Expression Rules

  • Must start with a number (can be negative: -3)
  • Then: Operator + Number, repeat
  • Example: 1 + 2 × 3 = 9 (left-to-right)
  • Basic operators: + − × ÷
  • Advanced operators (if enabled): % (modulo) ^ (power)
  • Parentheses ( ) can group sub-expressions

Special Cards

  • ★ Wild: Becomes any number you choose (1–99)
  • 🎯 Reroll: Changes the target number to a new random value
  • ×2 Double: Doubles your score for the next expression you score
  • 👁 Peek: Reveals the top 3 cards of the deck
  • 🔄 Swap: Exchange a card from your hand with one from your opponent

Tips

  • Unused Time = Bonus XP: Finishing your turn early gives you extra experience!
  • Use mass selection to quickly place multiple cards
  • Rehand is useful when you have a bad hand, but it ends your turn immediately
  • Host can customize which operators and special cards are in the deck

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • ESC / ? - Open/close this help
  • Space - Draw card (when it's your turn)
  • Enter - Try to score
  • Delete / Backspace / D - Discard selected card(s)
  • S - Sort your hand
  • U - Undo last playfield card
  • C - Clear playfield expression
  • Shift + Click - Add/remove card to selection
  • H - Toggle expression history panel
  • T - Toggle chat (multiplayer only)
  • Ctrl+Shift+C - Toggle cheat panel
